# Executive Project Brief

## Project Name
Customer Churn Prediction for Retention Strategy

## Business Objective
Develop a predictive analytics solution that identifies customers likely to churn so the business can take timely retention actions and reduce revenue loss.

## Why This Matters
Customer retention is a critical business priority for service industries. High churn rates reduce revenue, increase acquisition costs, and weaken customer lifetime value. Predictive churn analysis provides a data-driven way to target support and marketing actions toward the customers most likely to leave.

## Business Impact
This solution helps the business:

- reduce customer loss
- prioritize high-risk customer accounts
- improve retention campaign efficiency
- support strategic planning and customer success decisions
- strengthen overall revenue protection

## Project Scope
The project includes the full lifecycle of a practical data science solution:

- data preparation and validation
- exploratory data analysis
- preprocessing and feature engineering
- model comparison and evaluation
- SHAP-based explainability
- interactive prediction dashboard
- professional reporting and presentation assets

## Core Features
- Churn risk prediction for individual customers
- Interpretation of the most important predictors
- Metrics-driven evaluation of model accuracy and business usefulness
- Real-time prediction through a Streamlit application
- Presentation-ready documentation for stakeholders and recruiters

## Key Insights from the Model
The project demonstrates that churn is strongly influenced by factors including:

- shorter customer tenure
- month-to-month contract structures
- higher monthly charges
- internet service type
- technical support levels
- payment methods and billing behavior

These insights allow teams to understand why churn happens and where intervention should be focused.
